1. Who this policy is about

This policy covers the Android application Catch the Stones (package name com.zhuashizi.app), referred to below as "the app". It also covers the same game when played as a web page.

Effective date: 26 September 2026.

2. What data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts and no server component.

Data typeCollected?
Name, email address, phone numberNo
Location (precise or approximate)No
Device or advertising identifiersNo
Contacts, calendar, photos, filesNo
App usage, analytics, crash reportsNo
Purchase or payment informationNo — the app is free with no in-app purchases

Because nothing is collected, there is no data to request, correct, export, or delete on our side. There is no account to close.

3. Permissions

The app declares zero Android permissions. You can verify this yourself: the manifest contains no <uses-permission> element at all.

Two features are worth explaining, because similar apps usually do request permissions for them:

  • Haptic feedback uses the system's own view-level feedback API, which does not require the vibration permission.
  • Exporting your scores uses the system file picker, so the app never needs access to your storage or files.

4. Network access

The app makes no network requests of any kind, and the manifest does not declare the internet permission. The game is delivered entirely inside the installed app. If you play the web version, your browser fetches the page once from whatever host serves it; after that the game runs offline, and the host receives only the ordinary request information any web server sees.

5. Where your game data lives

Your scores, best runs and preferences are stored locally on your device only, in the app's private storage area.

  • Nothing is uploaded, synchronised, or backed up to us.
  • Uninstalling the app removes this data.
  • The app offers an optional export to a file you choose, so you can move your scores to another device yourself. That file is created at your request and stays wherever you put it.

6. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. There is no advertising, no social feature, no chat, no user-generated content, and no way for a child to communicate with anyone through the app. Because no personal information is collected at all, we do not knowingly hold personal information about children — or about anyone else.

7. Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or social SDKs. No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected. The Google Play Store itself may collect installation and crash information as part of distributing any app; that is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

8. Security

Since the app stores its data locally and transmits nothing, the main risk to your data is physical access to your device. The app adds one relevant safeguard: on startup it verifies the integrity of its own game files and shows a native error page rather than continuing if they have been altered.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. We do not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to act on. Your scores remain under your own control on your device, and you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change. The current version is unchanged in substance from the first release of the app.

三、权限

本应用声明的 Android 权限数量为 0。你可以自行核对：清单文件里完全没有 <uses-permission> 节点。

有两个功能值得说明，因为同类应用通常会为它们申请权限：

  • 触感反馈走的是系统本身的视图级反馈接口，不需要振动权限。
  • 导出成绩走系统文件选择器，因此本应用无需访问你的存储或文件。

四、网络访问

本应用不发起任何网络请求，清单文件里也没有声明联网权限。游戏本体完整地打包在安装包内。如果你玩的是网页版，浏览器会从托管方取一次页面，之后游戏完全离线运行；托管方只会收到任何网站都会收到的普通请求信息。

五、你的游戏数据存在哪里

你的成绩、最佳记录和偏好设置只保存在你自己的设备上，位于应用的私有存储目录。

  • 不会上传、不会同步、也不会由我们备份。
  • 卸载应用即会清除这些数据。
  • 应用提供可选的「导出为文件」功能，由你自行指定位置，方便你把成绩换到另一台设备。该文件由你主动创建，存放在你选择的位置。

七、第三方

本应用不包含任何第三方统计、广告、追踪、崩溃上报或社交 SDK。没有数据共享给第三方，因为没有数据被收集。Google Play 商店在分发任何应用时，自身可能会收集安装与崩溃信息，那部分适用 Google 自己的隐私政策，不属于本政策范围。

八、安全

由于本应用只在本地保存数据、不传输任何内容，你的数据面临的主要风险是设备被他人物理接触。应用为此加了一道相关防护：启动时会校验自身游戏文件的完整性，一旦被改动就显示原生错误页，而不是继续运行。

九、你的权利

GDPR、CCPA 等隐私法规赋予你查询、更正、删除、迁移个人数据以及反对处理的权利。我们没有持有你的任何个人数据，因此没有需要执行的操作。你的成绩始终由你自己在设备上掌控，随时可以通过清除应用数据或卸载来删除。
